Privacy policy

Effective 17 November 2026 · applies to the grail apps for Android, Windows and Linux, and to this website

grail keeps your notes on your device. It collects nothing and shares nothing, and it only goes online for the link previews you ask for.

Who makes grail

grail is made by MPKoutsoukos, an independent developer based in Greece, who is responsible for this policy. Contact: hello@grailnotebook.com.

What grail stores

Everything you make in grail (notes, tags, drawings, pictures you add, tables and snapshots) is saved as plain files on your device. On Android they live in grail's own private storage. Your settings, such as theme and reading layout, are stored the same way.

In the desktop app (Windows and Linux), the files live in the folder you choose as grail's home. If you choose a folder that a cloud service keeps in step, such as Google Drive for desktop, that service copies them under its own terms; grail itself sends nothing.

What grail collects

Nothing. grail has no account, no server, no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising and no tracking. The developer never receives your notes or any information about you or your device.

On Android, grail uses the internet permission only for link previews (below). It doesn't ask for the camera, the microphone, your location or your contacts. The desktop app likewise goes online only for link previews.

Link previews

grail only uses the internet to show link previews. When you ask for a preview of a link in a note (or for every link, if you turn link previews on in Settings), grail fetches that page from its website to show its title, a short description and its picture. For YouTube links it asks YouTube for its public description of the video instead. The preview is kept on your device, so the page isn't fetched again.

The website receives that request and your IP address, as it would for any visit, and handles it under its own privacy policy. The same goes for the picture, which some sites keep on a separate image server (YouTube's pictures come from YouTube's own image server). Nothing about your notes is sent, and nothing is ever sent to the developer.

Pictures

When you add a picture to a note on Android, you choose it with Android's photo picker. grail copies only the picture you pick into its own storage, and it can't see the rest of your photos.

Sharing and exporting

A notebook snapshot (Markdown or PDF) is saved inside grail. If you open a PDF snapshot, grail hands that one file to the PDF viewer you choose. Nothing leaves your device unless you send it somewhere yourself.

Backups

grail turns off Android's automatic cloud backup for its data, so your notes aren't copied to a backup service.

Deleting your data

Delete a note in the app and it's gone from your phone. Uninstalling grail, or clearing its storage in Android settings, deletes all of its notes. There is no copy anywhere else.

On the desktop, your notes are the files in your home folder: delete them there. Uninstalling the desktop app leaves that folder alone.

Your rights

Because grail and its developer hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for the developer to show you, correct or delete: everything is on your own device and under your control. If you have a question or a concern, write to hello@grailnotebook.com. If you are in the European Union, you also have the right to complain to a data protection authority; in Greece that is the Hellenic Data Protection Authority.

Children

grail isn't directed at children under 13, and it collects no data from anyone.

This website

grailnotebook.com is a static site hosted by GitHub Pages. It sets no cookies and uses no analytics or third-party scripts; its fonts are served from the site itself. GitHub, as the host, receives your IP address when you visit and may keep it in its logs for security, as described in the GitHub privacy statement. The download buttons take you to GitHub, and the video link to YouTube, under their own policies.

Changes

If this policy changes, for example if a future version adds optional sync, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date before that version is released.
